Everything a hackathon needs, in one place.

Participants and judges sign in to the same platform. Each sees only what their role needs.

Teams

Start a team or join one with an open seat. Up to four people, one leader, no spreadsheets.

Submissions

Upload slides, a PDF or a zip up to 20 MB, plus a repository link. Stored privately in S3.

Judging

Judges download each project and rate four criteria with sliders. The weighted total updates as they move.

Leaderboard

Rankings refresh every 15 seconds, so a judge's score reaches the big screen while the room watches.

Four steps from idea to ranking.

The same path for every team, from the first sign-in to the final leaderboard.

  1. 1

    Form a team

    Sign in with the account the organisers gave you, then create a team or join one with space.

  2. 2

    Submit your project

    One submission per team: a title, a short description, a file and an optional repository link.

  3. 3

    Judges score it

    Each judge rates innovation, technical depth, impact and presentation from 1 to 10.

  4. 4

    See where you rank

    Your average across judges goes on the leaderboard the moment a score is saved.

No black box. Just four weights.

Every judge scores the same four criteria. Your total is a weighted sum, and your rank is the average across all judges who scored you. Individual judges' scores stay private; the averages are public to everyone signed in.

Straight answers.

How do I get an account?

The organisers create your account and add you to the participants or judges group. Sign in with the email and password they send you; you'll set your own password the first time.

How big can a team be?

Up to four people. You can be in one team at a time. If the leader leaves, the longest-standing member takes over.

What can we submit?

One file up to 20 MB, such as slides, a PDF or a zip, plus a title, a description and an optional repository link.

Can we change our submission?

Yes: withdraw it and submit again. Do it before judging starts, because scores already given stay attached to the old submission.

How is the score calculated?

Each judge's total is 0.30 × innovation + 0.30 × technical depth + 0.25 × impact + 0.15 × presentation. Your leaderboard score is the average of those totals across judges.

Who can see the scores?

Everyone signed in sees the leaderboard averages. Only judges see individual scores and comments.
